SECTION 03 概要

ABOUT

Logosforge began with a refusal. A refusal to keep three apps open — one to write, one to plan, one to think — and to keep paying the tax of moving between them.

It is built as a narrative operating system: a single core where the manuscript, its structure and an engine-aware AI share the same memory of your story. Choose your engine and the whole system reconfigures around it. The AI doesn't guess what you're writing — it knows, because it reads from the same Story Bible you do.

Under the hood it's Python, Qt and a React surface over a local-first FastAPI core, with AI that runs on your own keys — local or cloud. No lock-in. No surveillance of your manuscript. Just the forge.
