Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 2026.06

Logosforge is built local-first. Your manuscripts, story bibles and project files live on your own device unless you explicitly enable sync — we never upload, read, sell or train on your writing.

What we collect

Only the minimum to run the service: your email if you create an account or buy a Pro license, and anonymous, opt-in diagnostics that help us fix crashes. Payment details are handled directly by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

AI requests

When you use cloud AI, the text you send goes to the model provider you choose, using your own API key. Logosforge does not retain or inspect those requests. Run a local model and nothing leaves your machine at all.

Cookies

This website uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any storage is strictly functional (e.g. remembering your license activation).

Your rights

You can request access to, export of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Email privacy@logosforge.app.
